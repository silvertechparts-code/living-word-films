Genesis 35: Back to Bethel.

At God's word Jacob returned to Bethel, after his household put away their foreign gods. God blessed him again and said his name is Israel. Rachel died giving birth to Benjamin, and Isaac died at a great age.

Let go of what you hold in God's place.
