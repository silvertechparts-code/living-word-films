Genesis 38: Judah and Tamar.

Judah kept his son Shelah from Tamar. Years later he did not know her when she met him on the road in a veil. When she was brought out to be burned, she sent him his seal, cord, and staff. He said: she is more in the right than I.

It takes courage to say: I was wrong.
