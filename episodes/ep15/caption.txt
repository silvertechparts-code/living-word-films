Genesis 15: Count the stars, if you can.

Abram had no child, but the Lord took him outside: count the stars, if you can. So shall your descendants be. Abram believed, and the Lord counted it to him as righteousness. That day the Lord made a covenant with him.

When your future looks dark, look up, and trust the One who made the stars.
