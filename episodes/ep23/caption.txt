Genesis 23: A place for Sarah.

When Sarah died at Hebron, Abraham mourned and wept. At the city gate he insisted on paying four hundred shekels of silver for the cave of Machpelah, and buried Sarah there.

Grief and faith stand together. Tears are not a lack of trust.
