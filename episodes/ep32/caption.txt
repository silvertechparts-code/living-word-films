Genesis 32: Wrestling with God.

Afraid to meet Esau, Jacob prayed and sent gifts ahead. Alone at the Jabbok, he wrestled with a man until daybreak and would not let go until he was blessed. He was named Israel, and he walked on, limping.

Struggle is not the end of faith.
