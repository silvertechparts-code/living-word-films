Genesis 20: A warning in a dream.

In Gerar, Abraham called Sarah his sister and King Abimelech took her. God warned Abimelech in a dream, Sarah was returned, and Abraham prayed and God healed the king's household.

God's faithfulness does not depend on our perfect record. Choose honesty, and trust him with the outcome.
