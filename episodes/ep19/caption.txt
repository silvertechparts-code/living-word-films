Genesis 19: Do not look back.

Two angels warned Lot, took him and his family by the hand and led them out of Sodom before fire fell on the city. Lot's wife looked back and became a pillar of salt, and God remembered Abraham.

Mercy pulls us out. Our part is to keep walking forward.
