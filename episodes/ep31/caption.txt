Genesis 31: Twenty years with Laban.

After twenty years with Laban, the Lord told Jacob to go home. Jacob left in secret, Laban chased him for seven days, and God warned Laban in a dream. They made a pact at a heap of stones, and Laban went home.

God sees the unfairness no one else notices.
