Genesis 21: Laughter, and a well in the wilderness.

God kept his promise and Sarah bore Isaac. When Hagar and Ishmael were sent away and the water ran out, God heard the boy crying, opened Hagar's eyes to a well, and was with him as he grew.

God keeps his promises, and he hears the cries of the forgotten.
