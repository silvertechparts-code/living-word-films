Genesis 11: A tower, a name, and a city that was never finished.

One people, one language, one plan: to build a city and a tower and make a name for themselves. The Lord came down, confused their speech and scattered them, and the place was called Babel.

Pride builds towers to be seen. Trust lets God do the building.
