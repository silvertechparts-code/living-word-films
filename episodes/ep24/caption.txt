Genesis 24: The servant at the well.

Abraham sent his oldest servant to find a wife for Isaac. At a well the servant prayed, Rebekah offered water for him and his camels, and when her family asked if she would go, she said: I will. Isaac loved her and was comforted after his mother's death.

God often leads us through ordinary kindness.
