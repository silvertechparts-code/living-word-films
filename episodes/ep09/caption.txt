Genesis 9: God set a bow in the clouds as the sign of his promise to the whole earth.

After the flood, God blessed Noah and his sons and made a covenant with them and with every living creature: never again would a flood destroy the earth. The sign was a bow in the clouds. Later, Noah planted a vineyard, and the chapter tells how his sons acted when he lay uncovered in his tent.

God's promise did not depend on Noah being perfect. Noah fell short, but the bow still stood. God's faithfulness is bigger than your worst day.
