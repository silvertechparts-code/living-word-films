Genesis 37: Sold by his brothers.

Jacob loved Joseph most, and his brothers hated him. After Joseph told them his dreams, they threw him into a pit and sold him to traders bound for Egypt. They sent his bloodied robe to Jacob, and Jacob mourned.

Deal with envy while it is small.
