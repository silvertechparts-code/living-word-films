Genesis 16: Hagar meets the God who sees.

When Sarai treated Hagar harshly, Hagar ran away into the wilderness. By a spring the angel of the Lord found her, promised her a son named Ishmael, and Hagar called the Lord: the God who sees me.

God sees the one everyone else overlooks, and he hears.
