Genesis 8: God remembered Noah, and the waters began to go down.

Noah's ark came to rest on the mountains of Ararat. Noah sent out a raven, then a dove, and he waited. One evening the dove came back with a fresh olive leaf. When the earth was dry, God told Noah to come out. The first thing he built? An altar.

God never forgot Noah, even through more than a year of waiting. Hope didn't come all at once. It came one dove and one small olive leaf at a time.
