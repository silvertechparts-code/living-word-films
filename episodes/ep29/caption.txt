Genesis 29: Seven years for Rachel.

At a well Jacob met Rachel and worked seven years for her. On the wedding night Laban gave him Leah instead, and Jacob worked seven more years. The Lord saw that Leah was unloved, and her fourth son was Judah: this time I will praise the Lord.

You are not defined by who chose you. God sees you.
