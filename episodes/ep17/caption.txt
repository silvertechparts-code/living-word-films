Genesis 17: Abram becomes Abraham.

At ninety-nine, Abram heard the Lord: I make my covenant with you. His name became Abraham, father of many nations, Sarai became Sarah, and God promised a son to be named Isaac.

God changed Abram's name before the promise came true. Believe what he says about you.
