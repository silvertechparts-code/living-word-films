Genesis 18: Three visitors, and a prayer for a city.

Abraham welcomed three visitors at Mamre, and the Lord promised that Sarah would have a son. Then Abraham stood before God and pleaded for Sodom, from fifty righteous people down to ten.

Prayer is not only asking for ourselves. It is standing before God for others.
