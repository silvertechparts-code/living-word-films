Genesis 26: The wells of Isaac.

In a famine the Lord told Isaac to stay in the land. When the Philistines filled in his father's wells, Isaac dug again. After two wells brought quarrels, he dug a third and named it Rehoboth: the Lord has made room for us.

Not every quarrel is worth winning. Choose peace, and trust God to make room.
