Genesis 13: Abram lets Lot choose, and chooses peace.

Abram and Lot became too rich to share the land. Abram offered Lot the first choice, and Lot chose the well-watered plain near Sodom. Then the Lord renewed his promise to Abram: look in every direction, all this land is yours.

Peace is worth more than winning, and trust is worth more than what we can see.
